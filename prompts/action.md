STYLE: Action
Suggest physical conflict or urgent physical action that grows out of the current situation: who or what could plausibly threaten, or be threatened, here, and what the characters could physically do about it. Use the actual surroundings (terrain, objects within reach, exits) and the characters' established abilities, gear, and injuries. Make the stakes immediate and the physical actions easy to follow. If the scene is calm, find the smallest believable spark instead of an attack out of nowhere.
