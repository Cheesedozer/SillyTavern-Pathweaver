STYLE: Mystery
Suggest clues, secrets, questions, or revelations connected to what the characters are trying to find out, or to something left unexplained in the recent messages. Early in a mystery, add clues and complications; later, connect existing clues or reveal part of the truth. Every clue must be something the characters could plausibly find here and now, and it should raise or narrow a specific question rather than hand over the whole answer.
